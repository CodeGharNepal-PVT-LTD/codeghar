About Suresh

Suresh is a co-founder of CodeGhar. He handles everything money-related — from monthly budgets to client invoices to making sure the company stays compliant with local regulations.

He's careful with numbers and transparent about them. Every major spending decision goes through Suresh, and he'll tell you straight if something doesn't add up financially.

He works closely with Gobinda and Subash to make sure ambitious project plans are also financially realistic — no overpromising, no surprise costs for clients.

Suresh also manages vendor relationships and contract terms. If you've ever wondered why CodeGhar's pricing is clear and upfront, he's a big reason why.
