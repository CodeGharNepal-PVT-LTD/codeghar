Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't you offer one-size-fits-all fixed packages?

Every software project has different requirements, user scales, and data complexities. A cookie-cutter price either overcharges for simple needs or underdelivers on critical engineering quality. We quote specifically for what your product requires.

How quickly can I get an estimate for my project?

Following our initial 30-minute discovery call and review of your requirements, we typically deliver an itemized technical proposal and timeline within 24 to 48 hours.

What payment structure do you follow?

For fixed-scope projects, payments are tied directly to verified milestone deliverables (e.g., Design Sign-off, Core Backend/Alpha, Beta, and Final Production Launch). For dedicated teams, engagement is billed on sprint or monthly cycles.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You have 100% ownership of all source code, models, and intellectual property developed for your project upon milestone settlement. We provide full repository access from Day 1.
