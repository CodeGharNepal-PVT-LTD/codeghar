The Code Ghar Story

कोड घरको कथा

Three of us started Code Ghar in Bhaktapur with a simple idea: Nepal deserves software as good as anything built abroad — without the agency price tag or the endless meetings.

हामी तीन जना मिलेर भक्तपुरमा Code Ghar सुरु गर्यौं। सोच थियो — नेपालले विदेशमा जस्तै राम्रो सफ्टवेयर पाउनु पर्छ, महंगो एजेन्सी र लामा बैठकहरू बिना।

Today we work with startups, local businesses, and anyone who needs a website, app, or internal tool built properly. We take rough ideas and turn them into working products. No fluff, no jargon — just solid engineering.

आज हामी स्टार्टअप, स्थानीय व्यवसाय र जसलाई पनि वेबसाइट, एप वा आन्तरिक उपकरण चाहिन्छ उनीहरूसँग काम गर्छौं। कच्चा विचारलाई काम गर्ने उत्पादनमा परिणत गर्छौं।

We are based in Bhaktapur Municipality-8, Libali. We believe technology should be accessible, affordable, and truly useful for Nepali businesses of all sizes.

हामी भक्तपुर नगरपालिका-८, लिबालीमा छौं। प्रविधि नेपालका सबै आकारका व्यवसायका लागि सुलभ र किफायती हुनुपर्छ भन्ने विश्वास गर्छौं।

Our Philosophy / हाम्रो दर्शन

Good technology should not need a manual. We build intelligent systems that are fast, intuitive, and designed to solve real everyday problems — from automated billing and customer assistants to clinic workflows and learning tools.
