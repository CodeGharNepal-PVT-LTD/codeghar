Mobile App Development

Android, iOS, or both — we build apps people actually keep on their phones. Design, development, and store submission included.

What We Deliver

Everything from the first sketch to the day your app goes live on the Play Store or App Store.

Android Development

Native Kotlin apps that work across different Android phones and OS versions — not just the latest Samsung.

iOS Development

Swift apps built to Apple's standards so they pass review and feel natural on iPhone and iPad.

Cross-Platform (Flutter)

One codebase for Android and iOS. Cheaper and faster if you need both platforms from day one.

UI/UX Design

We sketch the screens first and get your feedback before writing code. Saves everyone time later.

App Store Submission

We handle the Play Store and App Store paperwork, screenshots, and review process. Rejections included — we'll fix and resubmit.

Maintenance & Updates

OS updates break things. We fix bugs, patch security issues, and add features after launch if you need us to.

Our Platforms

We pick the platform based on your budget, timeline, and who your users are — not what's trendy.

Android

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Most of Nepal's mobile users are on Android — we build for that reality.

iOS

Swift and SwiftUI. If your customers use iPhones, we build apps that feel at home on iOS.

Flutter

One Dart codebase, two app stores. Good choice when you need Android and iOS but don't have the budget for two separate builds.
