Our Story

Under the vision and leadership of Founder & CEO Gobinda Bista, CodeGhar started with a simple belief: Nepal has world-class engineering talent, and businesses deserve technology built with transparency, precision, and genuine dedication. We didn't launch with big corporate investor backing; we began with deep engineering expertise, shared goals, and a lot of chai in Bhaktapur.

As Founder & CEO, Gobinda steers our overall technical architecture, company direction, and client partnerships, ensuring that every web application, mobile app, and AI system we deploy solves real business problems. We remain intentionally hands-on so that when you partner with CodeGhar, you work directly with passionate engineers who care deeply about your success.
