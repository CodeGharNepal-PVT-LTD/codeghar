Our Story

We did not start CodeGhar with a big investor or a fancy office. We started with a laptop, a shared Google Doc, and a lot of chai. The first few projects were for friends and local businesses who needed a website but could not afford Kathmandu agency prices.

Things grew slowly. We took on harder problems. We built real products for clients who trust us with their business systems. That trust is not something we take lightly.
