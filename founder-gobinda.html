About Gobinda Bista — Founder & CEO

Gobinda Bista is the Founder & CEO of CodeGhar Nepal Pvt. Ltd. He established CodeGhar with a straightforward mission: to use technology and AI to solve the small, real, everyday problems that local people, shops, clinics, and today's youth face — without throwing heavy buzzwords or charging unreasonable agency prices.

Solving Real Problems for Local People

Gobinda believes good technology should make life easier, not more complicated. Whether it's setting up an automated customer assistant so a local business never misses an inquiry at night, turning paper receipts into clean digital records with a smartphone photo, or building fast mobile apps that run smoothly on budget phones, his focus is always on practical daily utility.

Empowering Today's Generation

As a Computer Engineer currently pursuing his Master's in Computer Engineering (ME), Gobinda stays closely connected with youth, students, and new creators. He actively builds and shares practical AI tools for learning, exam preparation, and content creation, helping young Nepalis turn their digital ideas into real projects without needing big budgets.

Our Approach: Simple, Honest & Friendly

Gobinda's philosophy is simple:

  • No Heavy Jargon: We explain everything in plain language so you always know what you're paying for and how it helps.
  • Direct Developer Access: You talk directly with the engineers building your tool — no corporate middlemen or delay.
  • Honest Advice: If a free app or a simple fix already solves your issue, we will tell you straight and save your money.
"We aren't here to chase superficial tech buzzwords or ride hype cycles. My vision for CodeGhar is simple: build practical AI products right here in Bhaktapur that solve real, daily headaches for regular businesses, schools, and youth — while proving that world-class technology can be engineered in Nepal." — Gobinda Bista, Founder & CEO
